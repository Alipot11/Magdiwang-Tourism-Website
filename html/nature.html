Mount Guiting-Guiting

Above the clouds

Mount Guiting-Guiting rises 2,058 meters above the Sibuyan Sea, its jagged ridges often wrapped in mist. Climbers pass through mossy forest and open slopes to reach views that stretch across the island and the sea. Whether you trek to the summit or simply admire it from the coast, the mountain is the wild heart of Sibuyan.
