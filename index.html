MAGDIWANG

Tucked along the coast of Sibuyan Island in Romblon, Magdiwang is a place of slow mornings and wide horizons. Green mountains lean toward the Sibuyan Sea, rivers hum through the forest, and nine barangays welcome you with warmth. Come for the wild beauty and stay for the people.


Island hospitality

Mornings begin with the sea and the smell of coffee. In Magdiwang, life moves at the pace of the tide. Here, a stranger is greeted like family.


A town that celebrates

Magdiwang's name carries the spirit of celebration. And it shows each May during the Kasadyahan Festival, when streets fill with music, color and shared meals. Come join the dance, taste the local food, and leave with a story of your own.


The journey is part of the story

Magdiwang is reached by sea. Every route ends at the Port of Ambulong, where the adventure begins.


From Batangas Port

  1. Reach Batangas Port. From Manila, take a bus to Batangas Pier (about [2–3 hours]).
  2. Board the car ferry to Port of Ambulong, Magdiwang. It sails once daily and takes about 12 hours, so it is an overnight crossing. Bring a jacket and snacks.
  3. Arrive in Magdiwang and continue by [tricycle / van] to your stay.

Ferry operator: Starlite Ferries


From Lucena Port (Dalahican)

  1. Reach Lucena. Buses from Manila and nearby towns stop at Dalahican Terminal near the port.
  2. Board the car ferry to Port of Ambulong, Magdiwang, once daily, about 12 hours.
  3. Arrive in Magdiwang and continue by [tricycle / van].

Ferry operator: Starhorse Shipping


Travel tips

  • Check schedules with the ferry company before you go. Sailings depend on the weather.
  • Book early during holidays and festival season.
